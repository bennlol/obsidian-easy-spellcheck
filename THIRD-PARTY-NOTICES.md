## Downloaded dictionaries

No language dictionary is bundled with the plugin. The optional downloader retrieves selected files from a pinned revision of wooorm's normalized dictionaries collection. The collection tooling is MIT licensed, but every downloaded language retains its original copyright and license. The confirmation dialog links to those files before downloading.
